The Location

A privileged corner of the Costa del Sol

You're in Marbella, but completely out of it.

This is one of the last remaining green corridors on the Costa del Sol. River walks, country paths, an equestrian centre, and the prestigious Rio Real Golf Course on your doorstep.

The best beaches on this stretch of coast, including a dedicated dog beach. And the famous senda litoral, a wooden boardwalk that walks you straight into Marbella in 25 blissful minutes, with the sea beside you the whole way.

You can reach La Cañada shopping centre and central Marbella without touching the highway. For families, retirees, investors and anyone who wants the best of the coast without the noise, this is your area.
