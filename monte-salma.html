The Living Room

The first thing you notice as you walk in the door to this fifth floor golf penthouse is the luminosity throughout.

South-facing and generously proportioned, the living room offers approximately 40 m² of space, with marble flooring and an open outlook towards the golf course and Mediterranean beyond.

There's plenty of room for comfortable seating and a proper dining area, without compromising the sense of space.

The apartment has been well maintained over the years, with improvements to the bathrooms, appliances and air conditioning system. It is a home you could comfortably enjoy as it stands, although the generous proportions and straightforward layout also offer considerable scope for someone looking to introduce a more contemporary interior.

Cooking and Dining

The independent kitchen is fully furnished and equipped, with a practical layout that keeps cooking separate from the main living space.

For those who prefer an open-plan arrangement, the existing layout may offer possibilities for reconfiguration, subject to the necessary technical assessment.

But dining at this penthouse isn't limited to the interior. The outdoor terrace provides a wonderful setting for meals overlooking the golf course, while the rooftop solarium offers an entirely different experience with its own glass living room for cooler months.

Bedrooms

The penthouse has two generously sized bedrooms, both arranged on the same floor as the main living accommodation.

Built-in wardrobes provide practical storage, while the south-facing orientation and elevated position contribute to the apartment's bright, open character.

With two bedrooms rather than three, the property is particularly well suited to a couple, a smaller family or anyone looking for a Marbella home with space to accommodate visiting friends.

Bathrooms

Two bathrooms serve the apartment, both of which have benefited from updates over the years.

The property is well maintained and offers the opportunity for further personalisation without the immediate need for a complete renovation.

The Rooftop Solarium

This is undoubtedly one of the features that makes the penthouse stand out.

At approximately 116 m², the rooftop solarium is almost as large as the apartment itself. Accessed directly from the property's private terrace, it enjoys open views across Rio Real Golf towards the Mediterranean, with the mountains forming a backdrop to the surrounding landscape.

Part of the solarium has been transformed into a comfortable lounge area beneath a wooden-beamed structure, with double-insulated PVC glazing and awnings providing protection from the sun. It's a space designed to be enjoyed throughout the year, whether you're entertaining friends, reading in the afternoon or simply taking in the views.

There is also an outdoor dining area, making the rooftop a natural extension of the living accommodation.

An important detail: The solarium is community-owned but designated for the exclusive use of this penthouse. Buyers should review the relevant community documentation to confirm the precise terms of use.

The Community

The penthouse is situated within an established residential development dating from 1993, surrounded by mature communal gardens.

Residents enjoy access to a shared swimming pool, while the building benefits from lift access and the convenience of an included parking space. The community is well maintained and offers a peaceful residential setting, suitable for permanent living or longer stays in Marbella.

Living in Río Real

One of the pleasures of Río Real is how close you are to Marbella without feeling surrounded by the town.

The penthouse overlooks Río Real Golf — one of the Costa del Sol's most established and iconic golf courses, with its clubhouse, hotel and restaurant within walking distance.

The beach is approximately three minutes away by car or around 20 minutes on foot, where you'll find Trocadero Arena — a favourite for beachfront dining in Río Real. From here, the Senda Litoral coastal walkway offers a beautiful route along the Mediterranean towards Marbella.

Marbella town centre and La Cañada shopping centre are around five minutes away by car, making everyday errands, shopping and dining exceptionally convenient. It's this combination of a peaceful residential setting and proximity to everything that makes Río Real such an appealing place to live.
