Buying in Rio Real: What if you could buy a house for the price of a golf apartment?

With a budget of around half a million euros, many buyers looking at property in Rio Real automatically narrow their search to two-bedroom apartments. But look a little beyond the golf course and there are some rather interesting alternatives.

27 November 2026·By Louise Brace·7 min read

Lomas de Pozuelo, nestled in the green hills between Río Real and Marbella.

Rio Real has changed considerably over the past decade. Its proximity to Marbella, established residential communities and increasingly impressive collection of contemporary villas have positioned it firmly within Marbella East's mid to upper-end property market.

For buyers with a budget of around €500,000 to €550,000, the natural starting point is often an apartment in one of the established golf developments.

And there are some beautiful apartments to choose from.

But what if that same budget could buy you a three-bedroom house in a small residential enclave within walking distance of the beach?

What does half a million euros buy you in Rio Real today?

A look at the properties currently advertised for sale in Rio Real on Idealista and Fotocasa reveals some interesting comparisons.

Two-bedroom apartments in established developments around Rio Real Golf are being marketed at prices ranging from approximately €440,000 to €550,000 and considerably higher.

And we're not talking about new-build properties. Some of the apartments currently advertised at these prices were built in 2004 and 2005.

For buyers who enjoy apartment living, these developments offer plenty of appeal. Many have generous interiors, attractive communal gardens, swimming pools and underground parking, like this beautiful seaview apartment with roof terrace in Monte Salma complex.

However, for those relocating to Marbella permanently, working from home or buying with a growing family in mind, two bedrooms may involve a compromise that isn't entirely necessary.

Look beyond the golf course

Between Rio Real and Marbella lies Lomas de Pozuelo, a residential neighbourhood set among green hills and one that many prospective buyers are less familiar with.

Unlike some of the larger golf developments, Lomas de Pozuelo has a more residential character, with individual villas and small communities of townhouses.

Yet it shares many of the geographical advantages that make Rio Real such a desirable place to buy property.

Marbella town centre is around five minutes away by car (you don't need to get on the highway), the beach and Senda Litoral coastal boardwalk are within walking distance and La Cañada shopping centre is close by.

For those of us who live here, it's the proximity to Marbella combined with the feeling of living somewhere quite removed from the town that makes the location so appealing.

Las Lomas del Pozuelo — a quiet residential neighbourhood between Río Real and Marbella.

A different proposition at €530,000

Take Urbanisation Jardines de las Lomas, a small community of just 18 colourful, cottage-style townhouses arranged around mature gardens and a communal swimming pool.

One of these Rio Real townhouses is currently available exclusively through us at Rio Real Residences for €530,000.

Urbanisation Jardines de las Lomas, Lomas de Pozuelo — 18 townhouses arranged around mature communal gardens.

Behind its rather charming exterior is a generously proportioned three-bedroom home arranged across three floors.

The lower floor accommodates the main living areas, with a fireplace and sliding doors opening onto a 35 m² private terrace and the communal gardens beyond.

Two double bedrooms occupy the entrance level, while the entire upper floor is dedicated to a 27 m² master bedroom with its own bathroom and private balcony overlooking the surrounding hills and mountains.

There are two full bathrooms and a separate guest WC.

What the layout gives you is something that can be difficult to achieve in a two-bedroom apartment: separate spaces for different members of the household without sacrificing the main living accommodation.

The potential of these homes

I can speak about Urbanisation Jardines de las Lomas with some authority because I live here.

One of the things that first attracted me to these houses was the potential of their interiors. The original layout lends itself beautifully to a more contemporary way of living.

In my own home, I've opened up the lower floor to create a large open-plan kitchen, dining and living area that leads directly onto the terrace and gardens.

It has completely changed how I use the house, particularly when entertaining or spending time with family.

The top-floor bedroom, which I affectionately call my 'tower' bedroom, is another favourite feature. Having an entire floor to yourself is a rather lovely luxury.

These houses have their own architectural identity, and there's considerable scope to personalise the interiors without losing the character of the original home.

It's a different proposition from buying an apartment where much of the layout is already determined by the structure of the building.

Apartment or townhouse: Which suits your lifestyle?

There are very good reasons to buy a golf apartment in Rio Real.

Single-level living, lift access, secure parking and the convenience of a lock-up-and-leave property are attractive to many second-home owners and permanent residents.

But if your priorities include an additional bedroom, a dedicated space for working from home, your own front door and a terrace that opens directly onto a garden, a townhouse might be a better fit.

For the same budget, you could be buying a home that gives you more flexibility as your family or lifestyle changes.

And in a location where property prices can quickly move beyond the half-million-euro mark, that's an alternative worth exploring.

Sometimes it pays to rethink your property search

At Rio Real Residences, we often encourage buyers to look beyond their original property brief.

If you've been searching exclusively for two-bedroom apartments for sale in Rio Real, you may be overlooking some of the most interesting homes in the surrounding residential neighbourhoods.

Urbanisation Jardines de las Lomas is a particularly good example.

At €530,000, this three-bedroom townhouse occupies a price bracket shared by many two-bedroom resale apartments in Rio Real.

It's a home with its own personality, wonderful outdoor space and plenty of potential for someone who wants to put their own stamp on a property in Marbella East. It's also a great family home with safe gardens for children to play outside your back door.

And perhaps the most important question isn't whether you can afford to buy a house in Rio Real, but whether you've been looking in the right places.
