Who We Are

Specialists in one place, for one reason

Rio Real Residences & Living was founded by Louise Brace and Carlota Fernando — two women who have lived in Marbella for over twenty-two years, raised their children here, and know this corner of the Costa del Sol better than anyone selling property in it.

We met when our children started school together at the age of three. We've been friends since, have worked together in real estate, and both live in Lomas de Pozuelo, in the heart of the area we specialise in.

Louise Brace

Co-Founder

Louise has spent her career in digital marketing and holiday rentals, with years of experience as a buyer's agent in Andalucia and working for some of the coast's leading real estate companies.

She brings a deep understanding of how to market property — not just list it — and how to position homes for maximum visibility across search engines and AI platforms.

If you're an investor looking to enter the holiday rental market, Louise knows this environment inside out.

Our Approach

What makes us different

We are niche by choice. We don't pretend to cover the whole coast. We cover four neighbourhoods and we cover them completely.

We have direct relationships with many local owners, a genuine understanding of what makes each street and urbanisation different, and the marketing knowledge to present your property to exactly the right audience — wherever in the world they're searching.

We're not a typical estate agency. We're specialists and lifestyle advisors who happen to live where you want to buy.
